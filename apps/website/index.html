How It Works

Get started in three simple steps

1

Create Package

Register as a customer, create a package request with pickup and delivery locations.

2

Receive Bids

Verified drivers in your area will place bids. Choose the best offer for your needs.

3

Track & Deliver

Track your package in real-time and receive notifications when it's delivered.

For Drivers

Start earning by delivering packages

1

Register as Driver

Complete verification with ID and vehicle documents. Get verified to start receiving delivery requests.

2

Browse & Bid

View available package deliveries along your route. Place competitive bids on deliveries that match your schedule.

3

Deliver & Earn

Pick up packages, track your route, and complete deliveries. Get paid securely through the app.
